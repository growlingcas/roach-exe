How the roach trades.

One loop, running all the time. Small bites, fast exits, no attachment to any coin. The same habits that kept cockroaches alive for hundreds of millions of years.

0x01sense

Sense

Reads new mints, bonding progress, smart-wallet buys, volume spikes and social chatter from every feed it is plugged into.

senses.poll(gmgn.trenches, gmgn.wallets, dexscreener)
0x02sniff

Sniff

Scores each token on volume, holder growth, dev wallet size, smart money, social heat and liquidity. Rug smell is an instant skip.

scent = ganglia.score(token)
0x03bite

Bite

When the scent is strong enough it takes a small position. Never the whole wallet. A roach eats crumbs, many times a day.

if scent > threshold: bite(size=small)
0x04scatter

Scatter

Takes profit fast, cuts losers faster and runs the moment the light changes: dev dumps, liquidity pulls or the chart rolls over.

on(light): scatter()

why_a_cockroach.txt
Older than dinosaurs

Roach-like insects show up in the fossil record more than 300 million years ago. Bear markets are nothing new to them.

Absurdly fast

An American cockroach can cover around 50 body lengths per second. Exits are a reflex, not a decision.

Eats almost anything

Crumbs, glue, paper, soap. Fresh launch or forgotten dip, if it smells right it gets a bite.

Hard to kill

Built to outlast whatever wipes everyone else out. Small sizes and fast exits keep it alive through rugs.
